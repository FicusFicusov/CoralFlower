## CoralFlower 0.1: the first release

CoralFlower is free firmware that makes your M-VAVE FM-1 an instrument for chords. Hold a chord key, and every note
you play becomes a whole chord, with a bass underneath. While you play, a pixel flower grows from your chords. It is
inspired by the Telepathic Instruments Orchid, with controls redesigned for the FM-1's keys and buttons.

### What's in it

- The lower keys play the notes of your scale (26 scales, any starting note); hold an upper key, and each note
  becomes a chord. With no upper key held, single notes, several at once. Two key layouts, Top and Side, each with a
  Lock mode. Chord glide (off by default) slides each note into the next chord.
- A bass part under your chords (you can turn it off), with its own sound and range, plus an octaver that doubles each bass note.
- An arpeggiator with nine patterns, including a strum like the one on the Orchid chord synth. It locks to the
  metronome or to a MIDI clock.
- A looper for chord progressions, with count-in, overdub, undo and timing correction. Save the loop, and it stays
  after you switch off.
- Six effects in an order you choose: filter, drive, chorus, phaser, delay and reverb.
- About 60 virtual-analogue and FM sounds, with separate lists for chords and basses. Every sound has an editor, and you
  can save your own.
- A flower (Top layout) or a coral (Side layout) that grows from every chord.
- A short chime when the FM-1 starts. You can turn it off in the global settings (GLO > Display).

### Try it, then install it

The browser emulator runs this same firmware: <https://ficusficusov.github.io/CoralFlower/play/>

To install, open <https://ficusficusov.github.io/CoralFlower/> in Chrome or Edge on a computer. Connect the FM-1 with
a USB cable that carries data, without a hub, and press Install. It takes about a minute. If the FM-1 runs ChoralRoot or Felucca, the installer saves your sounds to a
file first. The official firmware's sounds can't be backed up. Installing firmware is at your own risk.
