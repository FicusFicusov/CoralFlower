## CoralFlower 0.1: the first release

CoralFlower turns the M-VAVE FM-1 into a chord instrument. Hold a root on the lower keys, add colour with the upper
keys, and the FM-1 plays the whole chord, in key and smoothly voiced, with a bass underneath. A pixel flower grows from
your chords as you play.

### What's in it

- Chords with two fingers in four layouts (Top, Side, and a Lock mode for each), quantized to 26 scales, with chord
  glide.
- A bass part with its own sound, octave and octaver.
- An arpeggiator with nine modes, including a strum like the Orchid chord synth's. It syncs to the metronome or to
  MIDI clock.
- A looper for chord progressions, with count-in, overdub, undo and timing quantize, saved to flash.
- Six effects in a chain you can reorder: filter, drive, chorus, phaser, delay and reverb.
- About 60 virtual-analog and FM sounds, with separate lists for chords and basses, full editors and your own saved
  sounds.
- A flower (Top layout) or a coral (Side layout) that grows from every chord.
- A short chime when the FM-1 starts. You can turn it off in GLO > Display.
