## CoralFlower 0.2: whole chords strike again

This update adds a new way to change chords and fixes glide on VA sounds.

### What's new

- **RETRIG: Chord.** A new setting in GLO > Play > RETRIG. When you change the chord while the last one still
  sounds, the whole new chord starts again with its attack, the notes the two chords share too. With a Glide time
  set, each note slides from the last chord's note.
- **Clearer RETRIG names.** The settings are now Wait, Slide (was Move), Notes (was Strike) and Chord. A choice you
  saved stays the same. With a Glide time set, Notes now slides the notes that change, as Chord does.
- **Glide on VA sounds.** Glide on virtual analogue sounds now takes the time you set. Before, it was much faster than the
  setting, so it was hard to hear.
- **A padlock in the top bar.** The Lock layouts show "Top" or "Side" with a padlock, so a long sound name has more
  room.

### Install

Open <https://ficusficusov.github.io/CoralFlower/> in Chrome or Edge on a computer. Connect the FM-1 with a USB cable
that carries data, without a hub, and press "Install on your FM-1". If the FM-1 runs CoralFlower, ChoralRoot or
Felucca, the installer saves your sounds to a file first. Installing firmware is at your own risk.

You can try 0.2 in the browser first: <https://ficusficusov.github.io/CoralFlower/play/>
