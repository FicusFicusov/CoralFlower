## CoralFlower 0.5: a sound for every pattern

In this update each pattern can have its own sounds, a HOLD key keeps your chords playing, and a MIDI keyboard can
play the FM-1's keys.

### What's new

- **A sound for every pattern.** On the SEQUENCER page, turn SOUND on the Chords, Bass or Layer row, or PRESETS, to
  give that pattern its own sound for the track; a row left on HOME plays the HOME sound. Patterns switch sounds at
  the bar line, the song plays each block with its pattern's sounds, and a copied pattern takes its sounds along. HOME
  shows a pattern's sound with its letter, such as "·C".
- **Sound edits stay in the pattern.** A change in EDIT or ENV to a pattern's sound stays in that pattern, no SAVE
  needed, for up to eight pattern sounds. SAVE turns the edit into a new sound.
- **Smooth sound changes.** When the next pattern brings a new sound, the last notes of the old one ring out while the
  new one starts. A chord you hold goes on in the new sound.
- **HOLD.** In the Top layouts, the last upper key is HOLD. Tap it, and your chord keeps playing after you let go. A
  new root replaces the held chord, a chord type key changes it on the same root, and a second tap on HOLD lets it go.
  The arpeggiator plays what HOLD holds. With no chord type lit, the keys play single notes.
- **Play from a MIDI keyboard.** GLO > MIDI > NOTES IN sets how a keyboard plays the FM-1, over USB or the 3.5 mm MIDI
  IN. "FM-1 keys" (the default) makes it play like the FM-1's own keys. "Roots" plays a root on every key while you
  pick chord types on the FM-1. "Direct" plays the chord sound and the bass note by note on their MIDI channels, as
  before.
- **GLIDE in the sequencer.** Each sound keeps its own GLIDE, and the sequencer's chords glide too, with the bass from
  the chords following them.
- **The flower plays along.** On HOME, the flower and the chord name follow the sequencer's chords. Hold some keys,
  and they show your chord instead.
- **ARP remembers.** The arpeggiator stays on or off after you switch off.
- **Move a chord right after a take.** Hold EDIT and turn SELECT works while the pattern plays, straight after you
  record.
- **Fuller pads.** A pad takes as many voices as the FM-1 has room for next to the other tracks: the less that plays,
  the more voices it gets. When room runs short, the tails of earlier chords go first.

### Fixed

- Playing right after you switch on, the boot sound steps aside at once and your first chord plays whole.
- On HOME, a long sound name no longer runs under the SEQ and ARP tags: it is shortened to fit, and the pattern's
  letter stays.

### Install

Open <https://ficusficusov.github.io/CoralFlower/> in Chrome or Edge on a computer. Connect the FM-1 with a USB cable
that carries data, without a hub, and press "Install on your FM-1". If the FM-1 runs CoralFlower, ChoralRoot or
Felucca, the installer saves your sounds to a file first; since 0.3.1 that file also holds your patterns. Updating
from 0.3.1 or later keeps your settings, sounds and patterns. If you had GLIDE set, your HOME sound takes it over as
an edit; SAVE the sound to keep it when you change PRESETS. Installing firmware is at your own risk.

You can try 0.5 in the browser first: <https://ficusficusov.github.io/CoralFlower/play/>
