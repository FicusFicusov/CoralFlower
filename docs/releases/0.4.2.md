## CoralFlower 0.4.2: everything as you left it

Switch the FM-1 off and on, and it's as you left it: your sounds with their edits, your settings, and PLAY ready
with the pattern or song you picked last. Chords also stay whole on heavy sounds, from the very first one.

### What's new

- **Everything as you left it.** Every setting is saved half a second after you stop changing it, even while a
  pattern plays or you hold a chord. That includes the bass you stop on in BASS SOUND and the boot sound switch.
  Patterns are saved as before, a moment after you stop PLAY.
- **Sound edits are kept too.** Changes to the chord, bass and Layer sounds in EDIT and ENV stay after you switch
  off, even if you didn't press SAVE.
- **PLAY picks up where you were.** After you switch on, the FM-1 starts on HOME, stopped. PLAY plays the pattern you
  picked last, or the song from the block under the frame.
- **Backups hold your sound edits.** A backup made on the site now saves your unsaved sound edits as well, and Restore
  brings them back.
- **QUANTIZE starts on Basic** on a new FM-1 and after a settings reset. Your own setting stays as it is.

### Fixed

- Fast chords on heavy FM sounds, such as CLAVINET, no longer drop to a single note.
- Right after you switch on, with the boot sound on, chords play whole from the first one. The boot sound is the
  same, only lighter on the FM-1, and it steps aside when your chords need the room.
- A very short tap on a chord type key is no longer missed.

### Install

Open <https://ficusficusov.github.io/CoralFlower/> in Chrome or Edge on a computer. Connect the FM-1 with a USB cable
that carries data, without a hub, and press "Install on your FM-1". If the FM-1 runs CoralFlower, ChoralRoot or
Felucca, the installer saves your sounds to a file first; since 0.3.1 that file also holds your patterns. Updating
from 0.3.1 or later keeps your settings, sounds and patterns. Installing firmware is at your own risk.

You can try 0.4.2 in the browser first: <https://ficusficusov.github.io/CoralFlower/play/>
