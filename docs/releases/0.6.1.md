## CoralFlower 0.6.1: every chord in the take

In this update a chord you play right after REC always makes it into the take, and REC on the rhythm machine counts you
in first.

### What's new

- **A count-in on the rhythm machine.** When a rhythm plays and you press REC on its page, the rhythm stops and one
  bar counts in (4-3-2-1 in 4/4). Then your take starts, and the rhythm starts again from its first bar with it. REC
  during the count-in calls it off. On HOME, REC under a playing rhythm still starts the take at once.

### Fixed

- **The chord you press with REC is in the take.** A chord you start after REC goes into the take, even when you press
  it almost together with REC, or let it go before the take starts. It used to get lost, often the first chord of a
  take. A chord you were already holding before REC stays out. This works on every page, for chords, bass and Layer
  notes.

### Install

Open <https://ficusficusov.github.io/CoralFlower/> in Chrome or Edge on a computer. Connect the FM-1 with a USB cable
that carries data, without a hub, and press "Install on your FM-1". If the FM-1 runs CoralFlower, ChoralRoot or
Felucca, the installer saves your sounds to a file first; since 0.3.1 that file also holds your patterns. Updating
from 0.3.1 or later keeps your settings, sounds and patterns. Installing firmware is at your own risk.

You can try 0.6.1 in the browser first: <https://ficusficusov.github.io/CoralFlower/play/>
