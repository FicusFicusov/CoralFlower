### CoralFlower 0.7: newer FM-1 support and new sequencer controls

This update adds support for the newer FM-1 flash chip and changes how you move around the sequencer. You can add a rhythm to a pattern you already recorded. The update also fixes an Autosave bug that could cause sound edits to disappear after you switched off.

**The SEQUENCER knobs have switched roles.** Turn SELECT to choose a pattern from A to H or open SONG. Turn ALGORITHM to select a track or a block in the song. To copy a pattern, hold OCT+, turn SELECT to choose where it goes, then let go.

#### What's new

- **Add a rhythm to a pattern you already have.** On the rhythm machine, hold OCT+ and turn SELECT to pick a pattern with an empty Drums track. Let go of OCT+ to copy the rhythm into that track. Your chords, bass and Layer notes stay in place. If a pattern or song is playing, the rhythm must match its time signature.
- **Clear notes and drum hits with SEL.** In the CHORDS, BASS and LAYER editors, tap SEL to clear the chord or note under the frame. Hold SEL until the screen shows the bars to clear, then let go to clear that track's active bars. POSITION sets the first bar and BARS sets how many bars are active. In DRUMS, a tap clears the selected drum in the bar under the frame. A hold and release clears that drum across its active bars and leaves the other drums in place. Hold REC to undo a clear.
- **Support for the newer FM-1 flash chip.** FM-1s with the UCUN UC25HQ80 chip can now keep sounds, settings and patterns after power-off and enter update mode from CoralFlower. It also supports the original Puya P25Q80H chip. CoralFlower leaves storage disabled if it does not recognize the chip.

#### Fixed

- **Autosave keeps sound edits after you switch off.** Edits could disappear after switching from a sound with a longer name to one with a shorter name. The fix also covers edits to sounds assigned to individual patterns.

#### Install

Open <https://ficusficusov.github.io/CoralFlower/> in Chrome or Edge on a computer. Connect the FM-1 directly with a USB data cable and press “Install on your FM-1”. To save a copy of your sounds and patterns before updating, use [Back up and restore](https://ficusficusov.github.io/CoralFlower/#backup).

If an older version of CoralFlower on a newer FM-1 won’t enter update mode, see the manual update steps under [Device not found, or the install stopped?](https://ficusficusov.github.io/CoralFlower/#dark)

You can try CoralFlower in the browser first. [Play it in your browser](https://ficusficusov.github.io/CoralFlower/play/)
