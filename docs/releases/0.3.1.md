## CoralFlower 0.3.1: patterns in the backup

This update adds the sequencer's patterns and song to the backup.

### What's new

- **Patterns and song in the backup.** A backup now holds the sequencer's eight patterns and its song, along with
  your sounds, settings and loop. Restore brings them back. Backups made with 0.3 don't have them.
- **Update first, then back up.** Updating to 0.3.1 doesn't touch the patterns saved on your FM-1. Before it
  installs, the page saves a backup by itself, but that backup comes from 0.3, so it has no patterns. To save your
  patterns, make a new backup after the update: open "Back up and restore" at the bottom of the page and press
  "Back up".
- **A reminder after installing.** When an install ends, the page says where to make a backup.

### Install

Open <https://ficusficusov.github.io/CoralFlower/> in Chrome or Edge on a computer. Connect the FM-1 with a USB cable
that carries data, without a hub, and press "Install on your FM-1". If the FM-1 runs CoralFlower, ChoralRoot or
Felucca, the installer saves your sounds to a file first. Installing firmware is at your own risk.

You can try 0.3.1 in the browser first: <https://ficusficusov.github.io/CoralFlower/play/>
