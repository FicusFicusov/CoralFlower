## CoralFlower 0.4.1: write while it plays

In this update you can write and move chords while a pattern plays, mute tracks in one pattern only, and switch to
the song on the fly.

### What's new

- **Write while it plays.** In a Chords, Bass or Layer track, the keys write into the step under the frame while the
  pattern plays, as they do when it's stopped. After a take, the frame hides, so you can play along with your loop
  without changing it. Turn SELECT or stop with PLAY, and the frame is back.
- **Move a chord.** Put the frame on a chord, hold EDIT and turn SELECT: the chord moves with the frame, and the top
  of the screen says "Moving". Let go of EDIT to drop it there. Hold REC for a second to put it back where it was.
  Works in the Chords, Bass and Layer tracks.
- **Hear the track you're on.** On the SEQUENCER page and in a track, the keys play the sound of the track under the
  frame: the Layer's own sound on Layer, a single bass note on Bass, and drums on the upper keys on the Drums track.
  PRESETS changes that track's sound, so you can pick a Layer sound by ear.
- **Mutes per pattern.** MUTE now belongs to each pattern: mute the drums in A, and they still play in B. A copied
  pattern keeps its mutes. A track you muted before the update stays muted in every pattern; from there, change them
  one pattern at a time.
- **Switch to the song while it plays.** With a pattern playing, turn ALGORITHM past H to SONG: the song starts from
  its first block at the next bar and loops. Turn back to a pattern, and it plays from the next bar.
- **PLAY on HOME plays the song** when the song was the last thing you picked, from the block under the frame. Open a
  pattern or record a take, and PLAY plays that pattern again.
- **Triplets are your call.** A take writes 16 steps a bar in 4/4 (12 in 3/4, 24 in 6/8). A bar whose STEPS you set
  keeps your grid, so for triplets in 4/4, set STEPS to 12 or 24 before you record.
- **A metal crash.** The crash cymbal rings like metal, with a long tail that softens at the end, in every kit.
- **A hint for KEY and SCALE.** When QUANTIZE is Off, KEY and SCALE are dimmed; turning one now says "QUANTIZE is Off:
  turn it on".

### Fixed

- Playing chords over a recorded loop no longer changes the take.
- The crash no longer stops when a closed hat plays.
- The drums no longer cut a note out of a chord you hold.
- In a busy mix, a Layer sound in unison gives way before the crash.

### Install

Open <https://ficusficusov.github.io/CoralFlower/> in Chrome or Edge on a computer. Connect the FM-1 with a USB cable
that carries data, without a hub, and press "Install on your FM-1". If the FM-1 runs CoralFlower, ChoralRoot or
Felucca, the installer saves your sounds to a file first; since 0.3.1 that file also holds your patterns. Installing
firmware is at your own risk.

You can try 0.4.1 in the browser first: <https://ficusficusov.github.io/CoralFlower/play/>
